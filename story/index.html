The Tide Between Us

A coastal community finds new ways to care for the water that carries them.

18 min • Published May 14, 2024

Video Player Placeholder

Transcript

Read along with the full story.

“At first light, the harbor is already awake. Nets move from boat to boat, and every conversation returns to the same question: what will we leave for the next tide?”

The sounds of the Atlantic are constant here—a rhythmic percussive backdrop to a life built on salt and scale. In this episode, we spend time with the families who have stayed, and those who are returning to redefine what it means to live in balance with the sea.

Fisherman Elias Thorne stands on the deck of his vessel, ‘The Marrow’, looking out at the horizon where the fog begins to lift. “It’s not about how much we take anymore,” he says, adjusting a thick rope. “It’s about how much we ensure is there for the ones who haven’t even been born yet.”
